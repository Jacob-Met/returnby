The shopper still reviews suggestions and supplies the confirmed return rule before saving.

## Top gaps

1. **Severely distorted print still hides some merchants and paid totals.** Lost lettering, stylized headers and glare on folded paper can leave both fields blank, even when a person can read the original.
2. **Some calendar apps retain obsolete reminders.** The desk lists outdated events and supports their cleanup, but external calendars can ignore cancellation files and require manual removal.
