# ReturnBy

**Current:** [0.8.0 — purchase to return trip](https://github.com/Jacob-Met/returnby/releases/tag/v0.8.0) · [source](https://github.com/Jacob-Met/returnby/tree/main)

**Real phone screen:** [saved return, packing and printable trip](https://github.com/Jacob-Met/returnby/releases/download/v0.8.0/ReturnBy-return-trip-phone.png)

**Top gaps**

1. A receipt photograph is still discarded after intake. Shoppers with paper-only receipts cannot reopen their original proof at the return counter from a saved purchase.
2. Conditional or conflicting merchant instructions still need human interpretation. Unfamiliar receipts can suggest an attractive wrong date; shipment versus merchant-arrival terms must never be inferred without evidence.
3. Phone packing still sits too far beneath trip selection and explanatory text. A returning shopper needs their next action and return document sooner, without scanning another long form.
