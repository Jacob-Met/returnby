# ReturnBy — current return desk

**[Download ReturnBy 0.9.3](https://github.com/Jacob-Met/returnby/releases/tag/v0.9.3)** · [Source](https://github.com/Jacob-Met/returnby/tree/main)

**[Phone: packing beside the selected trip](https://github.com/Jacob-Met/returnby/releases/download/v0.9.3/ReturnBy-v0.9.3-packing-phone.png)**

A returning shopper now reaches the saved return label, original receipt, packing checks and drop-off notes directly after choosing a trip, without passing the unselected order list. Desktop keeps packing alongside selection. The published Linux app preserved the working trip through reload and printable download at phone and desktop widths. Merchant shipment, drop-off and arrival-date review remains in the current release.

## Top gaps

1. **Two required handoffs still collapse into one reminder.** A merchant may require both carrier acceptance and warehouse arrival by different dates, but a purchase holds only one tracked deadline.
2. **Saved receipt photos are absent from JSON backups.** Moving to another device can leave restored purchases without their paper proof.
3. **Faint or distorted paper receipts still need manual reconstruction.** A shopper can lose important printed fields when their original photo is difficult to read.
